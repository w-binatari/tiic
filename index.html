About TIC

Building Nigeria's next generation of energy innovators

The Tech-Innovation Challenge (TIC) is a national innovation programme created by NCDMB to identify, nurture, and accelerate high-potential innovators developing solutions for Nigeria's oil, gas, and energy sectors.

Through screening, pitching, mentorship, bootcamp, and final showcase — TIC empowers innovators with the skills, exposure, and support needed to scale their ideas into impactful solutions.
