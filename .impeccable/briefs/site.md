# Surface brief: NCDMB TIC site (all pages)

Mode: Persuade on Home; Read on About, 2026 Edition and Contact.
Build path: code-led (direction pinned by the user's government-site anchor; no comp round).

## Direction contract

THESIS: TIC is the place where Nigerian lab work meets the oil and gas industry, so the site is about the innovators and their technology, not the regulator. It refuses the category default of a regulator crest over a stock building, a wall of equal icon cards, and Canva flyers pasted in as content.

OWN-WORLD: Deep NCDMB green fields (#0A3D24 ink-green, #006837 brand) owning whole bands; gold #F0B429 kept for the one action per view and for thin rules; pale mint #EAF3EC reading bands; white. Mulish 800–900 display set huge, tight and left-aligned; Lato for body text. Real event photography, full-bleed under a green multiply tint. Hairline gold rules, square corners, no blobs, no floating cards.

STORY: The visitor learns that TIC 2026 took 15 Nigerian technologies from lab to Finale Day, sees who the finalists are and what they built, believes that the judging and the backing (NCDMB, EICL) are serious, and registers interest for TIC 2027 or contacts the team.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed finale or judging photo under a dark-green tint. Bottom-left, a huge white two-line Mulish headline ("Nigerian research, built for industry."), one sentence of subcopy, a gold "Register interest for TIC 2027" button and a white text link "Meet the 15 finalists". A slim white header with the TIC logo, NCDMB crest small, and uppercase tracked nav, with a gold scroll-progress bar under it.

FORM: Government programme site per the BOA RH-SH Fund anchor (user-pinned), with the Africa Prize content shape (finalists and editions archive). Signature interaction: the finalists index. A dense ruled list of the 15 technologies by sector; selecting one opens its full profile in place. Motion: the scroll-progress bar, plus a one-time clip-reveal on section photos; nothing else moves. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
