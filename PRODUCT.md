# NCDMB Tech Innovation Challenge (TIC)

## What it is
A national programme of the Nigerian Content Development & Monitoring Board (NCDMB) that takes technologies from Nigerian universities, research institutes and young founders to market in the oil, gas and linkage sectors (Research to Commercialisation, R2C). It's delivered by Entrepreneurship & Innovation Centre Ltd (EICL), Port Harcourt.

## Where it stands (Nov 2026)
- The first edition, TIC 2026, is complete: Workshop (23 Sep 2025, Port Harcourt), then Call for Applications, Online Screening (8–18 Jun 2026), Physical Judging (30 Jun–2 Jul, NCDMB Towers, Yenagoa), Online Mentorship (13–31 Jul), R2C Bootcamp (3–7 Aug), Finale Day (10–12 Aug, NCDMB Towers), and Incubation for winners (24 Aug–13 Nov 2026).
- 15 finalists. **Winners have not been announced publicly**; never name or imply winners.
- **TIC 2027 is confirmed**; the production site states the next call for applications opens in December 2026. Never invent dates, prize amounts or numbers.

## Audience
1. Nigerian researchers, lecturers, polytechnic staff and early-stage founders deciding whether to apply to TIC 2027. They mostly browse on phones over mobile data.
2. Industry, investors and government stakeholders checking the programme's credibility and its finalists.
3. Press and partners looking for facts, photos and contacts.

## What the site must do
- Show that TIC is real and serious: the 2026 finalists, their technologies, the judges, the photos.
- Make it easy to register interest in TIC 2027 (a working form).
- Make it easy to contact the team (a working form; messages are emailed via Resend).

## Brand commitments
- NCDMB green #006837 and gold #F0B429. Both the NCDMB crest and the TIC logo must appear, but the crest is not the hero.
- Government-programme tone: plain, factual, confident. No hype, no startup slang.
- Design anchor: the user's government-site reference (Bank of Agriculture RH-SH Fund): full-bleed tinted photo hero with a big left-aligned headline, 50/50 photo/copy splits, a pale mint band, tracked uppercase nav, a solid CTA, and restrained motion (scroll-progress bar, light reveals).

## Constraints
- Fast on Nigerian mobile data: optimised images, self-hosted fonts, nothing that blocks rendering.
- Spelling: British/Nigerian ("commercialisation", "programme").
