Edition Overview

The 2026 Maiden Edition

The Maiden Edition of the Tech Innovation Challenge promotes Research to Commercialization (R2C), from lab to market, discovering and empowering Nigeria's brightest innovators in the energy sector.

Innovate · Accelerate · Commercialise · Succeed. Partnering for Progress. Advancing Nigerian Content, Driving Innovation.

  • Promoting Research to Commercialization (R2C)
  • Proposal online screening: 8–18 June 2026
  • Physical pitching for Top 30: 30 June – 2 July 2026 · Yenagoa
  • Physical bootcamp: 3–7 August 2026 · NCDMB Tower
  • Final TIC Event: 10–12 August 2026
  • Incubation for winners: 24 August – 13 November 2026
Programme Structure

Stages of the Challenge

Official TIC 2026 schedule, from proposal screening through incubation.

Stage 1 · Completed · 8–18 June 2026

Proposal Online Screening

Proposals are reviewed online to identify high-potential innovations for the next stage of the challenge.

Output: Innovators advance toward Top 30 physical pitching.

Stage 2 · Completed · 30 June – 2 July 2026

Judges’ Physical Pitching

Top 30 innovators pitch in person before judges at NCDMB Yenagoa.

Output: Competitors selected for coaching, bootcamp, and the Final TIC Event.

Concurrent · Ongoing · From 15 June 2026

Industry Engagement on TIC

Industry engagement for inputs and involvement starts 15 June 2026 and runs concurrently with the other programme stages.

Output: Stronger industry input and participation across the challenge.

Stage 3A · Ongoing · 13–31 July 2026

Online Coaching / Mentorship

Competitors receive structured online coaching and mentorship to refine their solutions, business models, and pitch readiness.

Output: Prepared for the physical bootcamp.

Stage 3B · Upcoming · 3–7 August 2026

Physical Bootcamp

Intensive physical bootcamp at NCDMB Tower, Yenagoa, with hands-on sessions, prototype refinement, pitch practice, and expert feedback.

Output: Final event readiness.

Stage 4 · Upcoming · 10–12 August 2026

Final TIC Event

Competitors present their solutions at the Final TIC Event. Winners are selected and awarded based on innovation, feasibility, and industry impact.

Output: Winners announced.

Post-Competition · Upcoming · 24 August – 13 November 2026

Incubation Phase for Winners

Winners receive product development support, access to NCDMB’s innovation space, funding pathways, and industry linkages to scale their solutions.
