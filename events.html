Edition Overview

The 2026 Maiden Edition

The maiden edition of the Tech Innovation Challenge promotes Research to Commercialization (R2C) — from lab to market — discovering and empowering Nigeria's brightest innovators in the energy sector.

Innovate · Accelerate · Commercialize · Succeed. Partnering for Progress. Advancing Nigerian Content, Driving Innovation.

  • Promoting Research to Commercialization (R2C)
  • Tech Transfer · Industry Partnerships · Funding · Business Strategy
  • Physical bootcamp at NCDMB Tower, Yenagoa
  • Grand finale: 3–5 August 2026
  • Post-competition incubation and funding support
Programme Structure

Stages of the Challenge

Each stage includes clear outputs and expectations for participants.

Screening Photo TBC

Stage 1

Screening

Entries are reviewed by an expert panel using a structured scoring rubric assessing technical merit, novelty, feasibility, and industry relevance.

Output: Top 30 innovators selected.

Pitching Photo TBC

Stage 2

Online Pitching

Shortlisted innovators pitch virtually before a panel of judges. The top 15 DCS competitors are selected based on clarity, innovation, impact potential, and scalability.

Output: 15 DCS competitors announced.

Mentorship Photo TBC

Stage 3A

Mentorship

Competitors undergo a four-week virtual mentorship programme covering business modelling, pitch refinement, technical development, and market strategy.

Output: Prepared for bootcamp.

Bootcamp Photo TBC

Stage 3B

Bootcamp

An intensive physical bootcamp at NCDMB Tower, Yenagoa featuring hands-on sessions, prototype refinement, pitch practice, and expert feedback.

Output: Final pitch readiness.

Finals Photo TBC

Stage 4 · 3–5 August 2026

Grand Finale

Competitors present their solutions to NCDMB leadership and a panel of judges at NCDMB Towers, Yenagoa. Winners are selected and awarded based on innovation, feasibility, and industry impact.

Output: Winners announced.

Incubation Photo TBC

Post-Competition

Incubation

Winners receive product development support, access to NCDMB's innovation space, funding pathways, and industry linkages to scale their solutions.
