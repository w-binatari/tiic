Edition Overview

The 2026 Maiden Edition

The 2026 Maiden Edition of the Tech-Innovation Challenge marks the beginning of a national journey to discover and empower Nigeria's brightest innovators in the energy sector.

This inaugural cycle brings together shortlisted innovators, finalists, mentors, and a distinguished panel of judges to shape the future of indigenous technology development.

  • Nationwide programme mobilisation
  • 4-week mentorship programme
  • 6-day physical bootcamp at NCDMB Tower
  • Grand finale and awards ceremony
  • Post-competition incubation and funding support
Programme Structure

Stages of the Challenge

Each stage includes clear outputs and expectations for participants.

Screening Photo TBC

Stage 1

Screening

Entries are reviewed by an 8-member expert panel using a structured scoring rubric assessing technical merit, novelty, feasibility, and industry relevance.

Output: Top 30 innovators selected.

Pitching Photo TBC

Stage 2

Online Pitching

Shortlisted innovators pitch virtually before a panel of judges. The top 15 finalists are selected based on clarity, innovation, impact potential, and scalability.

Output: 15 finalists announced.

Mentorship Photo TBC

Stage 3A

Mentorship

Finalists undergo a four-week virtual mentorship programme covering business modelling, pitch refinement, technical development, and market strategy.

Output: Finalists prepared for bootcamp.

Bootcamp Photo TBC

Stage 3B

Bootcamp

A six-day intensive physical bootcamp at NCDMB Tower, Yenagoa featuring hands-on sessions, prototype refinement, pitch practice, and expert feedback.

Output: Final pitch readiness.

Finals Photo TBC

Stage 4

Finals

Finalists present their solutions to NCDMB leadership and a panel of judges. Winners are selected and awarded based on innovation, feasibility, and industry impact.

Output: Winners announced.

Incubation Photo TBC

Post-Competition

Incubation

Winners receive product development support, access to NCDMB's innovation space, funding pathways, and industry linkages to scale their solutions.
