NCDMB Technology Innovation Challenge Workshop

The TIC journey opened with the NCDMB Technology Innovation Challenge Workshop. before Call for Applications, screening, physical judging, mentorship, Finale Day, incubation, and the Call for Applications in December.

NCDMB Technology Innovation Challenge Workshop

Theme: “Stimulating Industry & Economic Growth through Indigenous Technology Innovation”

Date: Tuesday, 23rd September 2025 Venue: Pavilion by Golden Tulip, GRA, Port Harcourt, Rivers State

Stakeholder and capacity-building workshop advancing Research to Commercialization ahead of Call for Applications and the competitive TIC programme.

Call for Applications

After the Workshop

Open call for innovators from universities, research institutions, and young entrepreneurs to submit proposals for the Tech Innovation Challenge.

Meet the 15 Finalist Innovations

Following several stages of competition, these 15 finalist teams advanced to the TIC 2026 Final Event, where they competed through three rounds of live judging for a place in the Top 3.

Showing 15 of 15

Team 01 TICC-2026-APP-028

Uthman B. Mohammed

Obsidian Gas and Energy Nig. Limited

Modular Capture-to-Treatment System: A Carbon-Assisted Water Remediation Solution for Nigerian Onshore Assets

A carbon-assisted modular system for water remediation tailored to Nigerian onshore oil and gas assets.

Team 02 TICC-2026-APP-031

Babson Ajibade

BMV-6 Medical Ventilator

A locally-made medical ventilator capable of independently ventilating multiple patients, designed for Nigerian healthcare conditions.

Team 03 TICC-2026-APP-057

Prof. Chidiebere Ugwu

AIoT-Enabled Clean Cooking Stove for Household Energy Management and Indoor Environmental Monitoring with Remote Dashboard

An AIoT-enabled clean cooking stove that manages household energy and monitors indoor air quality via a remote dashboard.

Team 04 TICC-2026-APP-059

Omar Umar Ahmed

Design, Fabrication, and Assembly of a Solid Oxide Electrolysis Cell (SOEC) for Efficient Hydrogen Production

Design and fabrication of a solid oxide electrolysis cell for efficient hydrogen production.

Team 05 TICC-2026-APP-013

Fossong Guilianno (team contact)

ACE-CEFOR 1 — World Bank Africa Centre of Excellence for Oilfield Chemicals Research, UNIPORT

Real Time AI-Driven Well Logging Analytics Platform for Enhanced Hydrocarbon Detection and Reservoir Characterization

An AI-powered platform that analyses well logging data in real time to flag hydrocarbon zones and support reservoir characterisation.

Team 06 TICC-2026-APP-025

Professor Olufemi M. Adesope

“FrassPlus” — An AI-Enabled, Low-Cost, BSF Frass, Green Technology for Hydrocarbon Bioremediation

An AI-enabled, low-cost Black Soldier Fly frass technology for hydrocarbon bioremediation.

Team 07 TICC-2026-APP-043

Prof. (Mrs.) Iheoma M. Adekunle

Local Material Substitution for Biostimulant Product Development Using Blended Invasive Aquatic Biomass for Remediation of Petroleum Hydrocarbon-Polluted Soils and Drilling Wastes

Biostimulants made from invasive aquatic biomass to remediate petroleum-polluted soils and drilling wastes.

Team 08 TICC-2026-APP-011

Saviour Henry

GeoPredict Technologies

GeoPredict AI: Intelligent Reservoir Characterization System — analyses seismic, well-log, and core data to speed up drilling decisions, flagging porosity, hydrocarbon presence, and promising drill sites

AI platform analysing seismic, well-log, and core data to accelerate drilling decisions and flag promising sites.

Team 09 TICC-2026-APP-058

Edward Ndiyo

Portable Energy System for Oil & Gas Field Operations and Commercial Use

A portable energy storage system built for rugged oilfield and commercial use in Nigerian conditions.

Team 10 TICC-2026-APP-067

Ngozi Inyang

DESA Nigeria

The Smart-Node IoT Framework: A Decentralized AI-Edge Solution for Real-Time Pipeline Integrity and Fluid-Flow Optimization

Decentralised AI-edge IoT nodes for real-time pipeline integrity and fluid-flow optimisation.

Team 11 TICC-2026-APP-075

Raphael Etaredafe

MaxFox AeroShield — AI-Driven Spectrum Monitoring and Digital Surveillance System

AI-powered RF spectrum monitoring and digital surveillance for infrastructure protection.

Team 12 TICC-2026-APP-005

Engr. Dr. Izionworu, Vincent Onuegbu

Rivers State University

MAR THERMO SIM (A Met-Ocean Corrosion Testing Unit) — recreates offshore heat, saltwater, and acidity conditions for testing protective coatings before deployment on platforms and FPSOs

A met-ocean corrosion testing unit recreating offshore heat, saltwater, and acidity for coating validation.

Team 13 TICC-2026-APP-041
