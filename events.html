Finalists Innovation Profiles

The 15 participants who qualified to advance to the Online Mentorship & Training ahead of Finale Day.

01

TIC-2026-APP-011

Saviour Henry / Anthony Obot

University of Uyo Alumni Innovation Initiative

GeoPredict AI: Intelligent Reservoir Characterization System for Enhanced Exploration Decision-Making

GeoPredict Technologies is building an AI-powered software platform that helps oil companies analyse geological and exploration data (seismic surveys, well logs, and core samples) to make better decisions about where to drill. Instead of taking weeks to process, the system delivers insights within hours, predicting rock porosity, hydrocarbon presence, and optimal drilling locations.

02

TIC-2026-APP-025

Professor Olufemi M. Adesope

University of Port Harcourt

Bio-Augmented Remediation of Petroleum-Impacted Soils and Organic Waste Upcycling using Black Soldier Fly Larvae Bioreactors

This project uses Black Soldier Fly larvae as natural cleaning agents to break down oil-contaminated soil and organic waste from oilfield camps. Raised in specially designed vertical trays, the larvae consume and digest toxic hydrocarbons in the soil over a 21-day cycle while processing food and organic waste, yielding high-protein larvae usable as animal feed and a nutrient-rich bio-fertilizer from the residue.

03

TIC-2026-APP-075

Etaredafe Raphael

MaxFox Technologies Ltd

MaxFox AeroShield: AI-Driven Spectrum Monitoring and Digital Surveillance System

AeroShield is an indigenous AI-powered radio frequency (RF) monitoring and surveillance platform. It uses a network of Software-Defined Radio (SDR) nodes to detect, locate, and intercept unauthorised VHF/UHF communications and UAV/drone signals used to coordinate pipeline vandalism and infrastructure sabotage, providing real-time alerts, direction finding, live audio interception, recording, and a secure command dashboard.

04

TIC-2026-APP-031

Babson Ajibade

Nigeria Content and Sustainable Technology (Nicost), University of Cross River State

BMV-6: Babson's Multiplex Ventilator

Babson Ajibade has designed and built a locally-made medical ventilator, the BMV-6, capable of independently ventilating up to six patients (newborns, children, and adults) simultaneously using a single machine. It runs on 24V DC power, can be powered by solar or wind energy, and has a built-in battery providing 33 hours of operation during outages. The touchscreen interface operates in Hausa, Igbo, Yoruba, Efik, Pidgin English, and English.

05

TIC-2026-APP-043

Prof. (Mrs.) Iheoma M. Adekunle

Environmental Remediation Research Group, Federal University Otuoke, Bayelsa State

Local Material Substitution for Biostimulant Product Development Using Blended Invasive Aquatic Biomass for Remediation of Petroleum Hydrocarbon-Polluted Soils in the Niger Delta

This project converts invasive aquatic weeds that clog Niger Delta waterways into soil cleanup products. The team harvests and processes multiple weed species into liquid and solid bio-stimulants that, when applied to oil-contaminated soil, activate natural microbes to break down petroleum hydrocarbons. Pilot studies in 2025 demonstrated up to 82% reduction in oil contamination within three weeks.

06

TIC-2026-APP-013

ACE-CEFOR, University of Port Harcourt

World Bank Africa Centre of Excellence for Oilfield Chemicals Research (ACE-CEFOR)

Real-Time AI-Driven Well Logging Analytics Platform for Enhanced Hydrocarbon Detection and Reservoir Characterization

ACE-CEFOR is building an AI-powered platform that analyses well logging data in real time as drilling proceeds. Sensors collect data on rock layers and fluid content as the drill goes deeper, and an edge computing device at the rig site processes this instantly, flagging hydrocarbon zones, identifying rock types, and alerting engineers to anomalies as they happen, rather than waiting until after drilling to interpret the data.

07

TIC-2026-APP-058

Speedlink Hi-tech Solutions Limited (Edward Ndiyo)

Speedlink Hi-tech Solutions Limited

Portable Energy Storage System for Oil & Gas Field Operations and Commercial Use

This project designs and assembles a portable lithium iron phosphate battery-based energy storage system with an integrated Battery Management System, pure sine wave inverter, and mobile monitoring. Built for rugged Nigerian conditions (dust, heat, humidity, and unstable grid power), it can be charged from solar or grid and provides clean, silent backup power, modular and available in different capacities.

08

TIC-2026-APP-067

Ngozi Inyang and Team

Digital Energy Systems & Analytics (DESA) Nigeria

The Smart-Node IoT Framework: A Decentralized AI-Edge Solution for Real-Time Pipeline Integrity and Fluid-Flow Optimization

This project develops a network of smart IoT nodes installed along pipelines. Each node uses piezoelectric sensors, edge AI (a convolutional neural network), and acoustic fingerprinting to detect tampering, drilling, or anomalies in real time. The system runs on LoRaWAN for long-range communication in remote areas, is solar-powered, and uses low-profile Stealth-Shell casings designed for Niger Delta conditions.

09

TIC-2026-APP-062

Abdul Nurudeen

Kaduna State Polytechnic

Design and Fabrication of a Bio-Hybrid Organic Rechargeable Battery (Diamagnetic Anisotropy Battery)

This project develops an eco-friendly, bio-hybrid rechargeable battery using locally sourced materials such as copper, aluminium hydroxide, cassava starch, and hibiscus-derived phenols. It creates a diamagnetic anisotropy effect at the electrode to improve energy retention and stability, combining battery-like storage with supercapacitor characteristics as a green alternative to lithium-ion and lead-acid batteries.

10

TIC-2026-APP-041

ACE-CEFOR, University of Port Harcourt

World Bank Africa Centre of Excellence for Oilfield Chemicals Research (ACE-CEFOR)

Optimisation and Commercial Deployment of Patented Green Oilfield Chemicals for Import Substitution in Nigeria's Oil and Gas Industry

ACE-CEFOR has already developed and patented six locally-made, eco-friendly oilfield chemicals over the past decade, including a drilling mud additive, corrosion inhibitor, pour point depressant, demulsifier, scale inhibitor, and gas hydrate inhibitor, derived from Nigerian plant-based raw materials. This project optimises these lab-proven products for real oilfield conditions and brings them to market as replacements for imported chemicals.

11

TIC-2026-APP-005

Engr. Dr. Izionworu, Vincent Onuegbu

Rivers State University

MAR THERMO SIM (A Met Ocean Corrosion Testing Unit)

Rivers State University has designed a laboratory testing machine, MAR THERMO SIM, that simulates the harsh ocean and heat conditions offshore oil structures such as FPSOs and platforms are exposed to. It replicates heat from gas flares, saltwater conditions, and varying acidity levels to test how well protective coatings and structural materials hold up before deployment offshore.

12

TIC-2026-APP-061

New Engineering Nig. Ltd. Team

New Engineering Nigeria Limited, NEWS Consult, ANDOLEF Manufacturing, University of Port Harcourt

The Nigerian Android Platform: A Solar-First, Smart Flare-to-Power Energy System (SF-HPU)

This is a modular 50 kW containerised hybrid microgrid combining solar PV, lithium-ion battery storage, and flare gas as a backup source, delivering over 99.5% power reliability for oilfield, industrial, and host-community use. Key innovations include AI-optimised control, a new locally-developed GEO-CERAMIC-NEWS material for battery insulation, and an open QwikConnect interface for integrating other Nigerian clean-energy technologies.

13

TIC-2026-APP-059

Omar Umar Ahmed and Team

Bayero University Kano, in partnership with the Hydrogen Energy Research Group, National Research Institute for Chemical Technology, Zaria

Design, Fabrication, and Assembly of a Solid Oxide Electrolysis Cell (SOEC) for Efficient Hydrogen Production

This project designs, fabricates, and assembles a Solid Oxide Electrolysis Cell using locally sourced materials. The system operates at high temperatures (700–900°C) to split steam into hydrogen and oxygen more efficiently than traditional low-temperature methods, and includes custom electrodes, electrolyte, manifolds, and a testing workstation for demonstration.

14

TIC-2026-APP-028

Obsidian Gas and Energy Nigeria Limited (O-GEN)

Obsidian Gas and Energy Nigeria Limited

Modular Capture-to-Treatment System: A Carbon-Assisted Water Remediation Solution for Nigerian Onshore Assets

O-GEN has engineered a skid-mounted modular unit that addresses gas flaring and produced water treatment simultaneously. It captures CO₂ from flared gas and injects it into alkaline produced water, naturally neutralising the water's pH, reducing the need for imported chemicals, and rendering the water clean enough for controlled discharge or dry-season irrigation. A pilot is planned for the Eremor Marginal Field, Bayelsa State.

15

TIC-2026-APP-057

Chidiebere Ugwu (Lead) and Team

University of Port Harcourt

AI-Driven Energy Management of Eco-Friendly Clean Cooking Stove to Power Electronic Devices in Underserved Communities

This project develops a smart, eco-friendly cooking stove that uses waste heat from cooking to generate electricity. It combines thermoelectric generators, an AI energy-management system, and a DC-AC inverter to provide both clean cooking and power for charging phones, small electronics, and household appliances (220V output), with the AI optimising how energy is shared between cooking and electricity generation.
