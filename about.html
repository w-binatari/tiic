About NCDMB TIC

Driving Research. Accelerating Innovation. Enabling Commercialisation.

The NCDMB Technology Innovation Challenge (TIC) is the Nigerian Content Development and Monitoring Board's flagship research and innovation programme dedicated to identifying, nurturing, and commercialising indigenous technologies that address critical challenges in Nigeria's oil and gas industry and its linkage sectors.

As the Board's premier innovation initiative, NCDMB TIC advances NCDMB's vision of strengthening local content by transforming high-potential research and innovative ideas into commercially viable technologies that create economic value, enhance industrial competitiveness, and deepen Nigeria's technological capabilities.

Our Purpose

Bridging Research and Commercial Success

Innovation has the greatest impact when it moves beyond the laboratory and into real-world application. NCDMB TIC was established to bridge the gap between research and commercial success by providing innovators with the structure, mentorship, industry engagement, and support needed to develop market-ready solutions.

The programme brings together researchers, startups, academia, industry professionals, technology developers, and entrepreneurs to solve real challenges facing the energy sector while creating opportunities for sustainable economic growth and indigenous technology development.

The Journey

From Research to Commercialisation

NCDMB TIC follows a structured innovation journey designed to help promising ideas become deployable solutions.

The programme begins with the NCDMB TIC Workshop, where prospective participants gain insight into the Board's priority challenge areas, evaluation criteria, programme expectations, and opportunities available throughout the challenge. The workshop equips innovators with the knowledge required to develop stronger, industry-relevant proposals while providing opportunities to engage directly with experts, mentors, and key stakeholders.

Following the workshop, the Call for Applications opened in December, inviting researchers, innovators, startups, research institutions, academia, industry suppliers, and members of the public to submit proposals aligned with NCDMB's approved thematic areas and priority industry challenges.

Selected participants progress through a rigorous evaluation process, receiving mentorship, business development support, and opportunities to refine their solutions. Outstanding innovations are further supported through incubation, investor engagement, and commercialisation within the NCDMB innovation ecosystem.

Focus Areas

Strategic Thematic Areas

The programme seeks innovative solutions across strategic thematic areas that address present and future industry needs.

  • Geological and Geophysical Studies
  • Local Materials Substitution
  • Technology Development
  • Health, Safety and Environmental (HSE) Studies
  • Engineering Studies
  • Renewable Energy

These focus areas are designed to stimulate practical, scalable solutions that strengthen Nigeria's energy industry while supporting broader industrial development and local capacity building.

Direction

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To build a thriving ecosystem where indigenous research and innovation translate into commercially successful technologies that strengthen Nigeria's oil and gas industry, stimulate economic growth, and position Nigerian innovators to compete globally.

Our Mission

To identify, support, develop, and accelerate innovative ideas through mentorship, industry collaboration, incubation, and commercialisation, enabling research-driven solutions to create measurable impact across the energy value chain.
